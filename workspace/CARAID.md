# CarAid – Mission & Operating Context

## 1. What CarAid Is

CarAid is building the infrastructure layer for vehicle damage intelligence.

Today, CarAid provides structured, photo-based repair estimates to car owners.
Users upload photos after an accident and receive a professional estimate—either free (slower) or paid (faster delivery).

**CarAid is not a body shop.**
**CarAid is not an insurance carrier.**
**CarAid sits between confusion and clarity.**

It translates damage into structured cost intelligence.

---

## 2. The Core Problem

After an accident:
- People don't know repair cost.
- Insurance estimates vary.
- Body shops create inconsistent estimates.
- Supplements inflate costs.
- Consumers lack transparency.
- Insurers lack standardized early signals.

There is friction, opacity, and delay.

**CarAid exists to reduce uncertainty at the earliest possible moment.**

---

## 3. Where CarAid Is Today (Phase 1 – Early Stage)

CarAid is currently:
- A solo-founder-led system
- Generating structured estimates from photos
- Offering:
  - Free 24–48h estimates
  - Paid 1-hour priority estimates
- Using AI + human review
- Monetizing lightly (small weekly revenue)
- Operating lean
- Building internal estimation rules and structured JSON schemas
- Exploring agent-based workflows

**Primary customer:** car owners
**Primary value:** clarity before going to insurance or a body shop

### Current constraints:
- Manual effort per estimate
- Limited scale
- No enterprise contracts
- Limited marketing budget
- No large recurring revenue

**This is validation stage.**

---

## 4. Near-Term Objectives (Next 3–6 Months)

CarAid must transition from:

**Tool → Service → Scalable Intelligence Platform**

### Priority Goals

1. **Increase Paid Conversion**
   - Improve delivery tier design
   - Improve urgency framing
   - Improve trust signals

2. **Reduce Human Time Per Estimate**
   - Standardized rule engine
   - AI pre-drafting
   - Better structured damage detection
   - Repeatable internal guidelines

3. **Strengthen Professional Positioning**
   - Clear service differentiation
   - Industry credibility
   - Clear market narrative

4. **Prepare for Capital Raise**
   - Clear market size story
   - Clear path to scale
   - Demonstrated traction
   - Compelling long-term vision

## Decision Framework for AI Agents

When working on CarAid tasks, prioritize:

1. **Clarity over complexity** – Simplify, don't over-engineer
2. **Speed to value** – Reduce manual effort, increase throughput
3. **Professional credibility** – Everything should feel trustworthy and industry-grade
4. **Lean operation** – Solo founder, limited resources, maximize leverage
5. **Validation mindset** – We're proving the model, not scaling prematurely

### What to optimize for:
- Faster estimate delivery
- Higher paid conversion
- Lower manual effort per estimate
- Stronger market positioning
- Clearer path to scale

### What NOT to do:
- Build features without clear ROI
- Over-complicate the service offering
- Pursue enterprise deals prematurely
- Spend on marketing without conversion optimization
- Add complexity that slows down estimates
